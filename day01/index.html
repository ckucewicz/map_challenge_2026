Closed Public Schools in Philadelphia (2003-2016)
#30DayMapChallenge · Day 1: Points
Data: School District of Philadelphia
About this map

Between 2003 and 2016, the School District of Philadelphia closed 75 public schools, including 24 in 2013 alone. Each dot is a closed school, colored by school level.

Closure year is the last school year a school appears in the district's longitudinal school list. Locations were geocoded from addresses, so some may be approximate.

Not every school here simply shut down. Some were taken over by charter operators, renamed, or merged, which makes them drop out of the district's list and show up as closures.
